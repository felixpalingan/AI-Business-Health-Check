THEORETICAL FOUNDATION

Why Adapted Balanced Scorecard (Kaplan & Norton)?

Connecting leading operational drivers to lagging financial outcomes

💰
1. Financial Health (35%)
Account segregation, cash runway, record-keeping method, and gross margin health.
⚙️
2. Operations (25%)
SOP documentation, key-person/owner dependency index, and inventory/waste control.
📊
3. Market & Client (20%)
Revenue concentration risk, channel diversification, and repeat customer tracking.
🏛️
4. Governance (20%)
Formal legal licensing (NIB/OSS), HR clarity, and performance-based incentive structures.
Academic Justification: Validated by a Systematic Literature Review (SLR 2022–2026) confirming BSC's efficacy as a feedforward management control system for small and medium enterprises.
FRAMEWORK COMPARISON

Why NOT the BCG Matrix?

Rigorous comparative analysis of popular strategic frameworks

Framework Core Analytical Focus MSME Applicability Verdict & Rationale
BCG Growth-Share Matrix Multi-product portfolio share vs. industry market growth ❌ Very Poor Eliminated. MSMEs sell 1-3 products and have zero market share data.
McKinsey 7S Organizational alignment (Strategy, Structure, Shared Values) ⚠️ Moderate Secondary. Highly qualitative, lacks numerical financial/cash runway benchmarks.
Weisbord 6-Box Organizational diagnosis (Purposes, Relationships, Rewards) ⚠️ Moderate Secondary. Excellent for team sociology, but ignores commercial liquidity mechanics.
Adapted Balanced Scorecard Multi-dimensional balance across Finance, Ops, Market & People ✅ Very High Adopted. Quantifiable, verifiable, comprehensive operational view.
REGULATORY FOUNDATION

Indonesian Regulatory Grounding (PP No. 7/2021)

Calibrated to legal MSME definitions and local banking standards

US-2 (KECIL)
Usaha Kecil
• Modal: > Rp 1 M — Rp 5 Miliar
• Omzet: > Rp 2 M — Rp 15 Miliar/thn
• Focus: Delegation, SOP, NIB, multi-channel.
US-3 (MENENGAH)
Usaha Menengah
• Modal: > Rp 5 M — Rp 10 Miliar
• Omzet: > Rp 15 M — Rp 50 Miliar/thn
• Focus: ERP, governance, formal audit.
Scope for Release v1: Focus exclusively on Usaha Mikro & Kecil in high-density sectors (F&B Kuliner, Retail Dagang, Personal Services) aligning with SAK EMKM financial standards.

SCORING ENGINE

Composite Scoring Formula & Tiers

Mathematical formula producing an intuitive 0–100 index

Total Score = (S_fin × 0.35) + (S_ops × 0.25) + (S_mkt × 0.20) + (S_gov × 0.20)
80 – 100
🟢 Healthy
Strong fundamentals, bankable, ready for expansion and formal credit facilities.
60 – 79
🟡 Fairly Healthy
Stable day-to-day operations, but specific structural gaps require remediation.
40 – 59
🟠 Needs Attention
Significant vulnerabilities present; vulnerable to sudden operational or cash shocks.
0 – 39
🔴 Critical
High distress risk; urgent intervention required to prevent insolvency.
DEEP DIVE: PILLARS 1 & 2

Financial Health & Operations Dimensions

Granular indicator breakdowns, weights, and scoring rules

💰 Financial Health (35% Weight)
Account Separation (25%) Separated = 100 · Mixed = 0 (🚨 P0 Flag)
Record-Keeping (25%) App/ERP = 100 · Spreadsheet = 75 · Manual = 40 · None = 0
Cash Runway (30%) ≥3 mo = 100 · 1-2.9 mo = 60 · <1 mo = 20 (🚨 P0 Flag)
Gross Margin (20%) Healthy per benchmark = 100 · Unknown/Thin = 20
⚙️ Operations & Efficiency (25% Weight)
SOP Documentation (35%) Written & active = 100 · Partial = 50 · None = 10
Owner Dependency (35%) Can leave ≥1 mo = 100 · 1 wk = 50 · ≤2 days = 0 (🚨 P1 Flag)
Inventory/Waste (30%) Stock opname = 100 · Receipts only = 40 · None = 0
DEEP DIVE: PILLARS 3 & 4

Market & Governance Dimensions

Protecting against customer vulnerability and legal non-compliance

📊 Market & Customer (20% Weight)
Revenue Concentration (50%) No single client >40% = 100 · Single client ≥50% = 30
Sales Channels (25%) Multi-channel (Online + Offline) = 100 · Single channel = 50
Repeat Order Tracking (25%) Actively recorded & measured = 100 · Untracked = 40
🏛️ Governance & Human Capital (20% Weight)
Business Licensing (50%) NIB + Sectoral Permit = 100 · NIB only = 60 · None = 10 (🚨 P2 Flag)
HR & Incentives (50%) Defined roles + performance bonus = 100 · Basic wage only = 50
SAFETY SYSTEM

Deterministic Red Flag Circuit Breakers

Rule-based emergency triggers executed before AI narrative generation

PRIORITY 0 (CRITICAL)
🔴 Liquidity Danger
Trigger: Mixed accounts == TRUE OR Cash Runway < 1 month.

Impact: High imminent risk of operational default or bankruptcy. Must be tackled in Week 1.
PRIORITY 1 (BOTTLENECK)
🟠 Operational Bottleneck
Trigger: Owner dependency ≤ 2 working days.

Impact: Founder burnout; zero enterprise valuation; inability to scale beyond founder hours.
PRIORITY 2 (COMPLIANCE)
🟡 Regulatory Exposure
Trigger: Operating > 1 year without NIB.

Impact: Barred from bank KUR loans, government procurement, and legal protection under PP 7/2021.
AI GOVERNANCE

Transparent Boundaries of the AI Layer

Clear demarcation of what the AI does versus what is strictly prohibited

✅ The AI DOES (Value-Add Synthesis) ❌ The AI DOES NOT (Strictly Prohibited)
Synthesizes contextual explanations tailored to the user's specific sector (F&B, Retail, Services) Does NOT calculate or modify scores; all formulas are hardcoded
Generates structured 30-Day Action Plans phased over Week 1 to Week 4 Does NOT trigger red flags; triggers are deterministic boolean rules
Maps findings to Indonesian standards (OSS-RBA, SAK EMKM, local KUR programs) Does NOT reclassify enterprise scale; strictly follows PP 7/2021 thresholds
Powers the Interactive Chat Advisor with full context injected Does NOT hallucinate financial data; constrained to diagnostic payload
